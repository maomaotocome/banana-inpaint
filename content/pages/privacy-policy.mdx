## Introduction

Welcome to BananaPro, the premier platform for **Nano Banana AI** image generation powered by Google's advanced AI models. At BananaPro, your privacy is of utmost importance to us, and this privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

This policy applies to the BananaPro website located at [bananapro.dev](https://bananapro.dev) and all related services, including our AI image generation tools, gallery features, and community platforms.

## Information Collection and Use

We collect the following types of data to provide you with a better experience while using BananaPro:

1. **Account Information**
   - **What We Collect**: This includes your name, email address, and any other information you provide when creating an account.
   - **Purpose**: To manage your account, provide customer support, and communicate with you about our services.

2. **Usage Details**
   - **What We Collect**: Information about how you use BananaPro, including your interactions with our AI image generation tools, prompts entered, images generated, features accessed, and usage frequency.
   - **Purpose**: To analyze user engagement, improve our AI services, and enhance the overall user experience.

3. **Generated Content**
   - **What We Collect**: Images generated through our platform, associated prompts, and metadata.
   - **Purpose**: To provide our services, improve AI model performance, and with your consent, showcase examples in our gallery.

4. **Device Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Data regarding the devices you use to access BananaPro, such as device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address.
   - **Purpose**: To optimize our services for different devices, ensure compatibility, and maintain security.

5. **Cookies and Tracking Technologies**
   - **What We Collect**: Small data files placed on your device that help us track user preferences and improve user experience.
   - **Purpose**: To enhance the functionality of our services, personalize your experience, and analyze site traffic.

6. **Payment and Billing Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Information regarding your payment methods, such as credit card number (processed securely by our payment providers), billing address, and any details required for transaction processing.
   - **Purpose**: To facilitate billing and payment processing for our subscription services.

## AI Image Generation Data

When you use our Nano Banana AI image generation features:

- **Prompts**: We store prompts you enter to generate images for service delivery and improvement purposes.
- **Generated Images**: Images are stored in your account and may be used to improve our services.
- **Model Interactions**: We log interactions with AI models for debugging, safety monitoring, and service optimization.

## Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. The data we collect is stored securely on our servers and with trusted cloud service providers. We implement a variety of security measures including:

- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication mechanisms
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
- Secure payment processing through PCI-compliant providers

However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties, except in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: To trusted third-party partners who assist us in operating our platform, including cloud hosting providers, payment processors, and analytics services, subject to confidentiality agreements.
- **AI Model Providers**: We may share limited data with AI model providers (such as Google) to enable image generation functionality, subject to their privacy policies.
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests from public authorities.
- **Protection of Rights**: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users or others.
- **Business Transfers**: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, or acquisition, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.
